Fit & details

Will these covers fit my L.A.B. DF3?
Yes. Every cover on this page is made for the L.A.B. DF3 and also fits the DF3i, with the opening in the center for the shaft. They fit other center-shafted mallets of a similar size too.
Will a regular mallet cover fit a DF3?
Usually not. Standard mallet covers, like our Spider mallet covers, have the opening at the heel, which leaves a center-shafted putter's neck exposed. Choose a center-shaft cover for the DF3.
How big are the DF3 covers?
About 4.625 in. long and 5 in. wide.
How do they close, and what's inside?
A magnetic closure keeps the cover on, and a soft velvet lining protects the putter's finish from scratches and dings.
What are they made of?
Most have a PU (synthetic leather) outer with embroidered artwork. The Corduroy uses corduroy and Sunday Stripes uses seersucker.
Can I return a cover?
